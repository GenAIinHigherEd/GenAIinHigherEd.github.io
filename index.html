GenAI in Higher Education Abstract Findings Team Funding
Impact of GenAI on Higher Education

A Novel Educational Assessment Framework for Enhancing Critical Thinking and Self-Efficacy via Generative AI Integration

Funded by Google.com via EMPIRE AI

Abstract

The multidisciplinary team at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI) is working to establish a novel and effective design instrument to assess the impact of generative AI (genAI) technologies on promoting learning and assisting students and faculty in upper-level undergraduate courses. This work aims to deploy a mixed-methods approach by utilizing approaches including surveys, large-scale data analysis to identify the nuances in student learning when relying on genAI. Our goal is to identify how students interact and get influenced by genAI in the context of academic self-efficacy, academic stress, and performance expectations.

Keywords: Generative AI  ;  Quantitative Investigation  ;  Survey Instrument  ;  Self-efficacy  ;  Natural Language Processing
